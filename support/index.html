Help for Go Clock Pro

Go Clock Pro is an offline game clock for go, baduk, weiqi, and igo. This page covers basic setup, purchases, and privacy expectations.

Email support Privacy policy

Common help topics

  • Start a game: choose a preset, or create a custom preset for both players before starting the clock.
  • Switch turns: tap the active player's half after making a move.
  • Background behavior: when the app is backgrounded or the device locks, the game pauses and saves a local snapshot.
  • Audio: countdown voice and beep behavior can be adjusted in Settings.
  • Language and appearance: app language, color mode, and clock size options can be adjusted in Settings.

Purchases and refunds

Go Clock Pro is a one-time paid app. It does not contain subscriptions, in-app purchases, ads, or tracking. Refund requests are handled by Apple through the App Store purchase system.
