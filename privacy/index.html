Privacy Policy

Go Clock Pro is an offline game clock. It does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data.

Data collection

Go Clock Pro does not collect personal data.

  • No accounts or login.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No third-party crash reporting SDKs.
  • No tracking SDKs.
  • No network service is required for gameplay.

Local data

The app stores game presets, appearance settings, audio settings, onboarding completion, tournament lock state, and the latest local game snapshot on the device. This data stays on the device and is not sent to the developer.

If the user has enabled Apple system backups such as iCloud Backup, local app data may be included in that Apple-managed backup.

Diagnostics

If users opt in to sharing diagnostics with Apple, Apple may provide aggregated crash and diagnostic information through App Store Connect or Xcode Organizer. This is handled by Apple diagnostics and is not a third-party SDK inside the app.
